SME Business Health & Insights Platform​
Domain: FinTech / Business Intelligence / Analytics

## Problem Description​
Small and medium businesses often operate without structured visibility into their financial
health, relying on fragmented tools or manual tracking methods that fail to provide actionable
insights. This results in poor financial planning, inefficiencies, and missed growth opportunities.
The challenge is to develop a system that enables small business owners to monitor, analyze,
and understand their business performance in a simple yet effective manner. The solution
should transform raw financial and operational data into meaningful insights that support better
decision-making. Teams may explore features such as revenue and expense tracking, trend
analysis, anomaly detection, cash flow insights, and operational performance indicators.


## Expected Deliverable​
A working prototype, such as a dashboard, web, or mobile application, that presents clear and
intuitive insights into business performance. The solution should highlight key financial trends,
detect anomalies, and provide actionable recommendations. It should enable small business
owners to make informed, data-driven decisions and improve overall financial and operational
efficiency.
